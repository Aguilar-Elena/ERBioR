# ERBioR source references

## ERBio method

Aguilar-Elena R. *Riesgos biológicos laborales: ERBio, un nuevo método de evaluación teórica*. Doctoral thesis, Universidad Pública de Navarra, 2015. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22069658.

The Zenodo DOI above is the preferred persistent reference for the underlying ERBio method in ERBioR.

## Regulatory and technical sources

- Spain: Real Decreto 664/1997, de 12 de mayo, consolidated text and Annex II: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/1997/05/12/664/con
- European Union: Directive 2000/54/EC, Annex III: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/54
- INSST BASEBiO: https://www.insst.es/agentes-biologicos-basebio

## Provenance policy

ERBioR separates the original ERBio method, Spanish legal classification, European concordance and BASEBiO technical enrichment. Technical data do not silently override legal classification. The package source manifest explicitly records which source layers have undergone structural or row-wise verification.
