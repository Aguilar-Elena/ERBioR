# ERBioR source references

## ERBio method

Aguilar-Elena R. *Riesgos biológicos laborales: ERBio, un nuevo método de evaluación teórica*. Doctoral thesis, Universidad Pública de Navarra, 2015. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22107465.

The Zenodo DOI above is the preferred persistent reference for the underlying ERBio method in ERBioR.

## Regulatory and technical sources

- Spain: Real Decreto 664/1997, de 12 de mayo, consolidated text and Annex II: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/1997/05/12/664/con
- European Union: Directive 2000/54/EC, Annex III: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/54
- INSST BASEBiO: https://www.insst.es/agentes-biologicos-basebio

## Provenance policy

ERBioR separates the original ERBio method, Spanish legal classification, European concordance and BASEBiO technical enrichment. Technical data do not silently override legal classification. The package source manifest explicitly records which source layers have undergone structural or row-wise verification.


## Scientific audit sources added in v0.7

- Spain. Orden TES/1287/2021, de 22 de noviembre, BOE-A-2021-19371. Official Annex II replacement used as the primary Spanish legal row source.
- Commission Directive (EU) 2019/1833, Annex III update to Directive 2000/54/EC.
- Commission Directive (EU) 2020/739, SARS-CoV-2 amendment.
- INSST BASEBiO technical database. Technical information never overrides the Spanish legal risk group.

## Persistent ERBio resources

### Original ERBio method

Aguilar-Elena, R. (2015). *Riesgos biológicos laborales: ERBio, un nuevo método de evaluación teórica*. Universidad Pública de Navarra.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22107465

This reference describes the original ERBio occupational biological-risk assessment method.

### ERBio core questionnaires and validation dataset

Aguilar-Elena, R. *ERBio — Core questionnaires for occupational biological risk assessment: the general assessment and worker perception instruments, with scoring algorithm and validation evidence.*

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22069658

This open dataset contains the core questionnaire instruments, scoring algorithm and validation evidence associated with ERBio.
