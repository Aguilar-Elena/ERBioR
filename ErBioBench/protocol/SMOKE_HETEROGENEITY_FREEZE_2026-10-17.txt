ERBIOBENCH — SMOKE HETEROGENEITY FREEZE
Date: 2026-10-17

STATUS
------
Exploratory heterogeneity and robustness analysis of the complete-pair
one-repetition smoke experiment.

Dataset:
96 complete model-case pairs
288 condition-specific observations

GLOBAL PAIRED EFFECTS
---------------------
C2 - C1:
mean = +2.165685 percentage points
median = 0

C3 - C1:
mean = +62.257981 percentage points
median = +60.97561

C3 - C2:
mean = +60.092296 percentage points
median = +60.97561

C3 CEILING PERFORMANCE
----------------------
87/96 complete model-case pairs achieved exactly 100% automated
accuracy under C3.

C3 perfect proportion = 90.625%.

Nine C3 pairs were non-perfect.

These consisted exclusively of:
- eight O3 responses with omission of the GRB field;
- one G2/P02 response with an incorrect GRB value.

All nine scored 90.2439%.

No additional C3 error family was identified.

MODEL-LEVEL ROBUSTNESS
----------------------
Mean C3 accuracy was 100% for 10 of 12 represented model slots.

Exceptions:
G2 = 96.74797%
O3 = 94.42509%

Leave-one-model-out ranges:

C2 - C1:
+1.738497 to +2.535437

C3 - C1:
+61.90804 to +62.54231

C3 - C2:
+59.78346 to +60.51403

Therefore, the large C3 effect was not driven by any single model.

CASE-LEVEL ROBUSTNESS
---------------------
Leave-one-case-out ranges:

C2 - C1:
+1.084011 to +2.614000

C3 - C1:
+61.67747 to +62.73035

C3 - C2:
+59.41762 to +61.64634

Therefore, the large C3 effect was not driven by any single benchmark case.

STRUCTURED PROMPTING
--------------------
The C2-C1 effect was substantially more heterogeneous across cases than
the C3 advantage.

Examples:
P09:  +18.3908 percentage points
P15:  +14.6341 percentage points, but based on only one complete pair
P04:   -2.1680 percentage points
P10:   -3.4146 percentage points
Several cases: 0 improvement

This is consistent with the paired inferential analysis in which the
mean C2-C1 difference was positive but the median and IQR of the paired
difference were both zero.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Structured prompting alone produced a small and heterogeneous benefit.

Deterministic ERBioR augmentation produced a large and highly
consistent improvement across models and cases.

The C3 effect remained approximately +60 percentage points relative to
C2 and +62 percentage points relative to C1 after exclusion of any
single model or case.

Case-specific estimates must be interpreted cautiously because the
number of complete pairs differs substantially between cases as a
result of refusals and technical failures.

This analysis is exploratory. Confirmatory heterogeneity analysis will
be based on the full repeated experiment.
