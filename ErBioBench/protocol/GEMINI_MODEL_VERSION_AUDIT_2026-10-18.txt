Interpretation
--------------

model identifier.

The requested model identifier must therefore remain unchanged in the
manifest and benchmark grouping.

However, the actual provider-reported model version must be retained as
an independent provenance field.

The observed mismatches are interpreted as provider-side alias/version
resolution and not as benchmark relabeling.
