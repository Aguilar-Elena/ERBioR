ERBIOBENCH — CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS PLAN
Full five-repetition experiment
Date: 2026-10-17

DESIGN
------
15 benchmark cases
12 model slots
3 experimental conditions
5 repetitions

Nominal runs:
15 × 12 × 3 × 5 = 2700

PRIMARY SCIENTIFIC POPULATION
-----------------------------
Scientifically valid model responses:
execution_status = COMPLETED
parse_valid_json = TRUE

Refusals and technical failures are analysed separately and are not
assigned zero accuracy in the primary scientific accuracy analysis.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
---------------
Automated accuracy according to the frozen ERBioBench scoring system.

Primary fixed effect:
condition
C1 = LLM only
C2 = structured prompt
C3 = LLM + deterministic ERBioR reference output

PRIMARY MODEL
-------------
Mixed-effects model accounting for repeated observations across:

- model slot;
- benchmark case;
- repetition.

The final random-effects structure will be simplified only if a component
has zero/negligible variance or produces a singular fit.

The condition effect will be tested globally.

Prespecified pairwise contrasts:
- C2 vs C1
- C3 vs C1
- C3 vs C2

Multiplicity:
Holm adjustment.

Reported:
- estimated mean difference;
- 95% confidence interval;
- adjusted p-value.

DISTRIBUTIONAL SENSITIVITY
--------------------------
Because accuracy is bounded and C3 may exhibit ceiling effects:

paired/non-parametric sensitivity analysis will accompany the mixed model.

SECONDARY SAFETY OUTCOME
------------------------
unsafe_assessment

Analyse:
- event frequency by condition;
- paired/clustered contrasts;
- exact methods when event counts are sparse;
- mixed logistic modelling only when estimable without separation.

SECONDARY PREVENTIVE OUTCOME
----------------------------
preventive_omission

Analyse:
- frequency by condition;
- paired/clustered comparisons;
- exact or mixed logistic methods as supported by the observed data.

REFUSAL OUTCOME
---------------
REFUSAL is a scientific behavioural outcome.

Report:
- refusal rate by condition;
- model;
- provider;
- benchmark case.

Over-refusal on legitimate occupational-health tasks will be analysed
separately from technical failure.

TECHNICAL ROBUSTNESS
--------------------
Technical outcomes are analysed separately:

- API/provider availability failure;
- quota/rate limiting;
- invalid JSON;
- output truncation;
- other transport failure.

Technical failures are not treated as incorrect scientific responses.

HETEROGENEITY
-------------
Prespecified exploratory heterogeneity analyses:

- effect by model;
- effect by case;
- effect by provider;
- leave-one-model-out;
- leave-one-case-out.

REPRODUCIBILITY
---------------
Across five repetitions evaluate:

- within-model consistency;
- condition-specific variability;
- frequency of exact agreement;
- variability of accuracy and safety outcomes across repetitions.

MISSINGNESS
-----------
No scientific score is imputed for:
- refusals;
- technical failures;
- runs never executed.

Complete-case analyses across conditions will be reported as sensitivity
analyses and not used to conceal differential refusal or technical
failure.

SCORING
-------
Frozen scorer:
score_llm_response_v3.R

Unsafe detector:
detect_unsafe_response_v4.R

No scoring rule will be changed after confirmatory execution begins
unless an objective software defect is identified, documented,
regression-tested and applied consistently to all affected observations.

INTERPRETIVE PRINCIPLE
----------------------
The primary scientific question is whether deterministic ERBioR
augmentation improves accuracy, safety and preventive completeness beyond
LLM-only and structured-prompt conditions.

Statistical significance will not substitute for effect-size
interpretation.

The smoke analysis is exploratory and does not constitute the
confirmatory result.
