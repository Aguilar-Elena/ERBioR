INTERPRETATION
--------------
Structured prompting alone produced a small average improvement over
LLM-only assessment. The median paired C2-C1 improvement was zero.

Integration with the deterministic ERBioR reference engine produced a
large and highly consistent improvement in automated accuracy and
eliminated observed preventive omissions in the complete-case smoke
subset.

Safety outcomes favored C3 descriptively but the smoke contained too
few unsafe events for statistically significant paired differences.

These results are exploratory and will not replace the confirmatory
analysis of the full repeated experiment.
