--------------------------------

- classified Brucella melitensis
- reference_level = 3
- regulatory_minimum_group = 3
- questionnaire compliance = 83.3333%
- 8 failed controls

2. Bilingual exposure scoring
--------------------------------
ERBioBench scoring now treats the fixed ERBio exposure categories
as semantically equivalent across Spanish and English:

Continuo = Continuous
Muy frecuente = Very frequent
Frecuente = Frequent
Irregular = Irregular
Ocasional = Occasional

No fuzzy matching or free translation is used.

This corrects the ERBB-P14 C3 score from 90.2439% to 100%
without changing the model response or making a new API call.
